# CoolBits.ai Progress Overview

This directory mirrors the AWB progress conventions and captures the heartbeat of the CoolBits.ai practice. Every document here is written for the founders, partners, and engineers who track our AI studio’s trajectory across Business, Agency, and DevOps pillars.

- **Governance:** describes the leadership hierarchy and clear responsibilities between CEO, Business, Agency, DevOps, and Andy as the human-in-the-loop layer.
- **Milestones:** records each strategic milestone from foundation through knowledge base ingestion, OpenAI chat engine rollouts, and downstream tokenization plans.
- **Plans:** outlines roadmap, architecture, and infrastructure plans that keep us deterministic and disciplined.
- **Notes:** daily logs, decisions, and design thinking, documented chronologically in AWB style.
- **Summaries:** weekly and monthly reflections that ensure transparency and remember what worked (and what needs course correction).

Use this structure to navigate our multi-language chat flow, safety guardrails, Camarad-backend convergence roadmap, and token-based consulting services.
