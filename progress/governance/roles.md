# Governance Roles

## CEO / Lead Strategist
- Maintains vision for CoolBits.ai as an AI consultancy that complements Camarad's legacy stack.
- Approves engagement tiers (exploration, focus, systems) and reviews budgets alongside legal/finance.
- Ensures Andy’s human-in-the-loop operations have the data, guardrails, and credibility they need.

## Business Automation Lead
- Conducts blunt discovery sessions to surface actual pain points and maps them to automation candidates.
- Designs process automations, knowledge assistants, intake flows, dashboards, and document workflows.
- Owns integration with CRMs, finance tools, and internal tooling, while aligning scope with agency and DevOps needs.

## Agency Services Lead
- Creates campaign automation helpers, reporting pipelines, and copilots for creative + analytics teams.
- Qualifies budgets, niches, creatives, and tracking maturity to stay within the specified engagement models.
- Keeps multi-language deliverables consistent with Revolving tone, ensures proposals respect platform constraints.

## DevOps & Engineering Lead
- Builds gateways (chat.js + router), handles model selection, logs, monitoring, rate limits, and token economics.
- Maintains infrastructure plan, Terraform/CICD alignments, and ensures safe model usage across Business/Agency requests.
- Drives safe fallback logic for jailbreaks, braking, and anti-spam policies, ensuring Andy remains calm, professional, and purposeful.

## Andy (Front Desk Alias)
- Serves as the persona that the chat assistant uses when talking to clients across all verticals.
- Encourages context-specific questions, qualifies budgets, and redirects off-topic requests back to the pillars above.
- Adds disclaimers about supervision, acknowledges human oversight, and preserves tone consistency regardless of language.
