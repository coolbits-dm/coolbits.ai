# Governance Hierarchy

| Layer | Responsibility | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CEO / Lead Strategist** | Sets studio-level goals, client portfolio focus, financial cadence, and partnerships (including future Camarad-root integrations). | Maintains Andy alias oversight and approves multi-language safety policies. |
| **Business Pillar Lead** | Oversees automation projects for operations, customer support, dashboards, knowledge workflows, and ensures the AI proxies respect contract constraints. | Coordinates with DevOps on data access and ensures agency teams can productize outputs. |
| **Agency Pillar Lead** | Owns paid-ads automation, reporting pipelines, and creative copilots; ensures deliverables align with multi-language, compliance-safe flows. | Partners with Business for shared analytics and DevOps for tracking instrumentation. |
| **DevOps Pillar Lead** | Architects infrastructure (VPS, GCP, Cloud Run), pipelines, intent routing, observability for AI usage, and drives the token economy. | Enforces guardrails and orchestrates the central gateway (the `app/server` stack). |
| **Andy (Alias)** | Human-in-the-loop front desk that owns tone, safety, and redirection. Andy is guided by these pillars and is the persona the assistant uses to respond. | Andy ensures clients feel like a single partner even though Business/Agency/DevOps teams are specialized.

The hierarchy blends strategy, automation, and engineering to keep CoolBits.ai focused, compliant, purposeful, and ready for eventual integration with Camarad components where needed.
