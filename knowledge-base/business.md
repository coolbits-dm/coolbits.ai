# CoolBits for Business

CoolBits.ai is an AI development studio that helps businesses turn messy, manual processes into reliable, automated systems.  
We do not sell “AI magic” or generic chatbots. We design and build very specific, very practical tools that your team can actually use.

---

## What we build for business clients

Typical solutions include:

- **AI-powered assistants for operations**  
  Answer internal questions, assist with procedures, summarize documents, prepare reports, and help staff navigate tools and policies.

- **Workflow and process automation**  
  Automate repetitive tasks such as data entry, cross-system updates, approvals, reporting, follow-ups, and handoffs between teams.

- **Customer support and intake flows**  
  Smart intake forms and assistants that collect the right details from customers, triage tickets, and route requests into your existing systems.

- **Decision support dashboards**  
  Combine data from multiple tools (CRMs, marketing platforms, accounting, internal trackers) and surface simple, actionable views and alerts.

- **Document, contract, and knowledge workflows**  
  Classify, tag, summarize, and route documents. Create “knowledge assistants” that answer questions based on your rules and content, not random web data.

We start from your existing tools and constraints and design systems that respect them, instead of forcing you into yet another platform.

## Typical engagement levels

We usually work in three tiers:

### 1. Exploration & Prototype
- **Scope:** One department, one use case, or one painful workflow.  
- **Duration:** ~3–7 business days.  
- **Investment (rough range):** €800 – €2,500.  
- **Outcome:**  
  - A working prototype or proof-of-concept.  
  - A short technical and business summary.  
  - Clear “go / no-go” next steps.

### 2. Focused Implementation
- **Scope:** One to three connected workflows, or a small internal “AI assistant” used by a team.  
- **Duration:** ~3–5 weeks.  
- **Investment (rough range):** €3,000 – €10,000.  
- **Outcome:**  
  - Deployed solution integrated with your existing tools (where possible).  
  - Dashboards and basic monitoring.  
  - Documentation and handover session.

### 3. Systems & Long-term Evolution
- **Scope:** Cross-team automation, multiple assistants, reporting and decision support across departments.  
- **Duration:** ~2–4 months, possibly longer.  
- **Investment (rough range):** €12,000 – €40,000+.  
- **Outcome:**  
  - A stable, evolving “AI layer” on top of your business.  
  - Governance rules, access control, logging.  
  - Ongoing refinement based on real usage.

These ranges are not quotes. They exist to help you see if our type of work matches your budget and expectations.

## How we work

1. **Clarify the pain.**  
   We ask blunt questions until the real problem is visible in plain language.

2. **Map your tools and constraints.**  
   We list what systems you already use, what must stay, what can change, and where we can insert automation.

3. **Propose a minimal, concrete solution.**  
   No platforms, no buzzwords. Just “this is what we will build, here is how it behaves, here is what it needs from you.”

4. **Implement in small, visible steps.**  
   You see progress early. We do not disappear for months.

5. **Measure, refine, and decide together.**  
   If something does not help in practice, we change it or remove it. The point is impact, not “features”.

## When we are not a good fit

- You only want “an AI chatbot on the website” with no real integration or process change.  
- You expect guaranteed ROI numbers before any discovery work.  
- You want us to plug into a fragile legacy system with no possibility of improvement.  

In those cases, we will be honest and say no.
