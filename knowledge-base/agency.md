# CoolBits for Agencies

CoolBits.ai works with digital, performance, and creative agencies that want to use AI as infrastructure, not as a gimmick.  
We focus on building tools that reduce manual work, improve consistency, and create new service lines you can offer to your clients.

We do not replace your team. We build systems that make your best people faster and more precise.

---

## What we build for agencies

Typical solutions include:

- **Campaign automation helpers**  
  Generate and refresh ad copy, search themes, headlines, and descriptions aligned with brand rules and platform limits.

- **Reporting and analytics pipelines**  
  Automatically collect data from Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, analytics tools, and CRMs to produce clear dashboards and client-ready summaries.

- **Account health monitors**  
  Track budget pacing, conversion anomalies, search term issues, and structural problems, then surface them in a single view.

- **Client-facing AI copilots**  
  Simple, guarded assistants that can answer “what is happening”, “what did you change”, “what should we expect”, without exposing internal systems.

- **Internal knowledge copilots**  
  Help new team members understand your playbooks, checklists, and account standards by querying a controlled knowledge base.

## Engagement models

### 1. Internal tools for your agency
- **Duration:** ~1–3 weeks for a focused assistant or script bundle.  
- **Investment:** ~€1,000 – €5,000 depending on depth and integrations.  
- **Goal:** Give your team a tangible productivity edge on existing accounts.

### 2. White-label services for your clients
- **Duration:** ~3–6 weeks for a repeatable “productized” service.  
- **Investment:** ~€3,000 – €12,000.  
- **Goal:** Create something you can sell again and again across your portfolio (e.g., AI audit, AI reporting kit, conversion insights layer).

### 3. Full AI-augmented agency stack
- **Duration:** multi-phase over 2–4 months.  
- **Investment:** typically €15,000+.  
- **Goal:** Transform the agency’s internal operations and client-facing reporting into an AI-enabled system.

## How we work with agencies

- We respect your **position in front of the client**. You remain the face.  
- We work as a **technical partner in the background**, not as competition.  
- We agree up front on what is reusable across clients and what is bespoke.  
- We document what we build so your team can maintain and extend it.

---

## What we do NOT do for agencies

- We do not take over your clients or pitch directly to them behind your back.  
- We do not sell “secret hacks” or guarantee specific CPM/CPC/ROAS.  
- We do not push you into a platform you do not control.

We are interested in long-term collaboration, not one-off tricks.
