# CoolBits — Frequently Asked Questions

## Are you a marketing agency?

No. We are an AI development studio.  
We have strong experience with marketing and analytics, but we build systems and tools, not campaign packages or media buying retainers.

We often collaborate with agencies and internal marketing teams instead of replacing them.

---

## Do you only work with AI projects?

Our core focus is where AI, automation, and existing systems meet.  
Some projects involve heavy LLM usage, others lean more on classic automation, data processing, or scripting.

If we can solve your problem reliably without AI, we will say so.

---

## What does a typical first project look like?

Usually:

1. A short discovery conversation.  
2. A written summary of your context and constraints.  
3. A small, well-defined build (one workflow, one assistant, or one “slice” of a bigger idea).

We prefer to start small, deliver something real, and grow from there.

---

## How much does it cost to work with you?

It depends on scope and risk.  
As a rough orientation (not a quote):

- Small, focused builds: ~€800 – €3,000.  
- Medium projects: ~€3,000 – €12,000.  
- Larger, multi-phase systems: €12,000+.

We do not lock you into recurring fees by default. Ongoing work and support are discussed explicitly.

---

## Do you build long-term products or only prototypes?

We prefer to build things that survive beyond the prototype stage.  
In some cases, a prototype is the right first step; in others, it makes sense to build a minimal but production-ready system from day one.

We are candid about which option makes sense for your case.

---

## Can you integrate with our existing tools?

In many cases, yes.  
We typically integrate with CRMs, analytics tools, ad platforms, ticketing systems, internal databases, and custom APIs.

We will ask you to be clear about what is allowed, what is off-limits, and what security rules apply.

---

## Do you offer support after delivery?

Yes, but not as an automatic subscription.  
Support, maintenance, and evolution can be:

- bundled for a period after delivery, or  
- structured as a separate, ongoing collaboration.

We prefer to be transparent about effort and cost instead of hiding it.

---

## How do we get started?

The simplest way is to describe:

- what you do,  
- what feels slow, manual, or fragile,  
- and what a “good outcome” would look like in very simple terms.

From there, we can tell you if it is a fit and propose a concrete first step.
