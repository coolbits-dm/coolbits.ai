# CoolBits for Dev & DevOps

CoolBits.ai works with technical teams that need help turning AI experiments into stable, observable systems.  
We sit in the overlap between software engineering, DevOps, and applied AI.

We are not a replacement for your team. We are a force multiplier when you have more ideas than time.

---

## What we build for dev / devops clients

- **Lightweight AI gateways and proxies**  
  Centralized entry points to OpenAI and other providers: logging, routing, cost control, model selection, and basic policy enforcement.

- **RAG and retrieval layers**  
  Practical retrieval-augmented generation systems for internal knowledge, support, documentation, and analysis. We focus on simplicity and maintainability.

- **Pipelines and workers**  
  Background jobs for processing data, generating content, syncing tools, and running periodic checks on systems or metrics.

- **Monitoring and observability for AI usage**  
  Metrics, logs, and dashboards to understand where tokens, errors, and time go.

- **Dev tooling and internal consoles**  
  Small, focused tools that give your team visibility and control over your AI components.

## Typical engagement ranges

### 1. Targeted technical projects
- **Examples:**  
  - AI gateway with logging and basic policies.  
  - Simple RAG stack for a well-scoped corpus.  
  - Job worker that calls LLMs at scale.
- **Duration:** ~1–3 weeks.  
- **Investment:** ~€2,000 – €7,000.

### 2. End-to-end feature builds
- **Examples:**  
  - New AI-powered feature inside your product.  
  - Integration of LLM calls with your existing backend and front-end.  
  - Cost and performance optimization of an existing AI workload.
- **Duration:** ~3–8 weeks.  
- **Investment:** ~€6,000 – €25,000 depending on scope.

### 3. Long-term AI / DevOps partnership
- **Scope:** Ongoing evolution of your AI stack, including new services, migrations, monitoring, and incident support (not 24/7).  
- **Duration:** multi-month.  
- **Investment:** negotiated as a retainer or rolling project work.

All ranges are indicative. Final numbers depend on clarity of scope, tech stack, and constraints.
