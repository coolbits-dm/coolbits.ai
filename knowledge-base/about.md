# About CoolBits.ai

CoolBits.ai is an independent AI development studio.  
We design, build, and maintain small, sharp, realistic tools that sit inside your existing business, not above it.

We are not a “platform” or a generic SaaS product.  
Every project is scoped with you, for your context, with clear boundaries.

---

## How we see our role

- **Translator:** turn vague ideas (“we should automate this”) into concrete, buildable systems.  
- **Engineer:** design and implement the technical stack needed to support those systems.  
- **Partner:** stay involved long enough to see what actually works in your daily operations.

We are comfortable in the space where marketing, operations, and engineering overlap.

---

## Human in the loop

CoolBits.ai is built and operated by humans who use AI heavily, not the other way around.

“Andy” is the working alias we use for the assistant that speaks on behalf of the studio.  
Andy is not a single person and not a fully autonomous entity.  
Responses are generated with AI, guided by rules and by the studio’s experience.

Where it matters (contracts, architecture decisions, budgets, risk), a human is always in the loop.

---

## Where we work

We work primarily remotely, with clients that are comfortable collaborating via async communication and focused calls.  
Time zones and locations matter less than clarity and shared expectations.

If you need a local, always-on, on-site team, we are not the right fit.  
If you need a focused, precise partner for your AI and automation work, we might be.
