    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 2025-12-24

    This Privacy Policy explains how CoolBits.ai (“CoolBits”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services (the “Service”).

    1. Scope

    This policy applies to the Service, including Chat, connector dashboards, snapshots, and any other features that may be offered from time to time.

    2. Information we collect

    • Account data: email address and profile information you provide.
    • Usage data: feature usage, pages viewed, actions taken, timestamps, and diagnostic events (e.g., errors) required to operate the Service.
    • Customer inputs: prompts, messages, and files you submit in Chat or other tools, and the outputs generated for you.
    • Connector configuration: workspace selections, customer IDs, and configuration metadata needed to fetch reporting data from third-party platforms.
    • Technical data: IP address (approximate), device/browser information, and cookies or similar identifiers.

    3. Connected accounts and third‑party data

    When you connect third‑party services (for example Google), we access and process the data you authorize in order to provide requested reports and insights. Third‑party providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

    What Google data we access

    • Google Ads (reporting data): performance metrics and breakdowns such as cost, clicks, impressions, CTR, average CPC, conversions, conversion value, ROAS/CPA, campaign-level performance, device, network, and (when enabled) search terms and keyword performance.
    • Google Analytics 4 (reporting data): traffic and engagement metrics such as users, sessions, events, conversions, revenue; and common dimensions used in dashboards such as page/path, source/medium, geo (country/city/region), and device category.

    4. Google API data & Limited Use

    When you connect Google services (Google Ads and/or Google Analytics 4), CoolBits accesses and processes Google user data only to provide features you request (connector dashboards, reporting, snapshots, and automated insights inside the Service).

    CoolBits’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

    We do not use Google user data to serve ads, build advertising profiles, or sell/transfer data to data brokers. We do not allow third parties to use Google user data for their own purposes.

    We only share Google user data (a) with Google APIs as needed to retrieve the reporting data you request, and (b) with vetted service providers that process data on our behalf for hosting, security, and reliability, under confidentiality and security obligations.

    Human access to Google user data is restricted to what is necessary for customer support, debugging, fraud prevention, and security investigations.

    5. How we use information

    • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
    • Authenticate users and secure accounts.
    • Generate dashboards, reports, and automated insights you request.
    • Operate billing, subscriptions, and account management.
    • Monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and debug issues.
    • Communicate with you about important changes, security updates, and support.

    6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

    Where applicable, we process information based on one or more of: (a) performance of a contract (providing the Service); (b) legitimate interests (security, reliability, and improving the Service); (c) your consent (where required); and/or (d) compliance with legal obligations.

    7. Sharing

    We may share information with:

    • Service providers that help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics, billing).
    • Third‑party platforms you connect, to the extent needed to request and retrieve data you authorize.
    • Legal and safety: when required to comply with law or protect rights and safety.

    8. Data retention

    We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention may vary by data type (e.g., billing records may be retained longer than transient logs).

    9. Revocation, disconnect, and deletion

    • Disconnecting Google: You can disconnect Google Ads or GA4 at any time from the Service’s connector settings. When disconnected, we stop calling Google APIs for that connector and remove stored OAuth tokens for that connector.
    • Revoking in Google: You can also revoke CoolBits’ access from your Google Account settings. If access is revoked, connector reporting will stop until you reconnect.
    • Deletion requests: You may request export or deletion of personal data and connector-related data via /contact.
    • Snapshots and reports: Snapshots/reports generated inside the Service may be retained in your workspace until you delete them or request deletion, subject to legal and billing record retention requirements.

    10. Security

    We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials.

    11. Your choices and rights

    • Access, update, or delete certain account information in your account settings (where available).
    • Request export or deletion of personal data, subject to legal requirements.
    • Object to or restrict certain processing in jurisdictions where these rights apply.

    12. Cookies

    We may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and operate the Service. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

    13. Children

    The Service is not directed to children and should not be used by anyone under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

    14. Changes

    We may update this policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to provide notice (for example by posting an update on this page).

    15. Data controller

    Data controller: COOL BITS S.R.L. (Romania). For privacy questions or requests, use /contact (select “Privacy” topic) and we will respond.
